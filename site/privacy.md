# Privacy Policy (draft)

This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- No account, no sign-in.
- No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
- Your bookmarks, progress and settings stay on your device.
- Audio is downloaded from the King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex or our mirror servers only when you
  press play or download. No personal data is sent.
- The microphone (optional "Listen" feature) is used only when you tap it; audio is processed on your device and
  is never saved or uploaded.
